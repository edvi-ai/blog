## How to Actually Shortlist Universities in Germany

If you follow the steps, it is actually not too difficult.

- **Pick your exact field:** Not an *extremely specific field* like "Computer Science." 
But rather, choose the specific direction: AI, Data Science, Embedded Systems, Media Informatics, and so on. 
This removes most irrelevant programs.

- **Choose the language of study:** English taught only, or both English and German. Your list shrinks immediately.

- **Match your subjects and ECTS:** Check each program's required subjects. If your transcript covers at least seventy percent of them, it is a good match.

- **Check GPA requirements:** Top unis often look for 1.0 to 2.0. Many strong public universities accept 2.5. Applied sciences universities are more flexible.

- **Look at your background:** Your bachelor's branch and your work experience should align with the program. 
*Missing core subjects is the main reason for rejections.*

- **Consider your practical needs:** Deadlines, documents, required tests, city preference, and cost of living all influence your final list.

- **Build a balanced shortlist:** *Some ambitious programs, a few safe ones* - that's your mantra. This setup gives you high acceptance odds.
That is it.
